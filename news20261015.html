Owning Our Stories in the Age of AI - AAPI Trailblazers Conference

By UCACF • October 15th, 2026 • 2 min read

I'm reaching out on behalf of the Serica Initiative to invite you to join us for our 2026 AAPI Trailblazers Conference — Owning Our Stories in the Age of AI, taking place on Wednesday, November 4, 2026, in New York City.

This year's conference will bring together AAPI leaders, innovators, entrepreneurs, and creatives to explore how artificial intelligence is reshaping our communities, industries, and future opportunities. Through thought-provoking conversations and interactive sessions, we'll examine AI's impact on leadership, representation, storytelling, and career readiness, while highlighting the importance of AAPI voices in shaping this rapidly evolving technology.

We would love to engage your organization as a community partner and extend the following invitations:

  • Two complimentary conference tickets for your organization's leadership or representatives.
  • 20% discounted tickets for your members and broader network who are interested in attending.
