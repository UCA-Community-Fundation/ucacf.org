🌟 本月焦点｜UCACF影响力招待会圆满举行

6月29日，UCACF在第五届美国华人大会期间举办影响力招待会。捐赠人、受助机构代表、UCACF理事及社区领袖齐聚一堂，共同回顾UCACF资助项目在全美各地带来的社区影响。

招待会介绍了UCACF的发展方向及基金申请计划，多位受助项目代表也分享了项目成果和社区故事。此次活动既是对长期支持者和合作伙伴的感谢，也为各地社区组织建立联系、交流经验和探索合作创造了机会。

每一笔资助的背后，都是一个社区创意从想法走向行动的开始。UCACF期待继续连接更多捐赠人、公益组织和社区伙伴，让更多有意义的项目落地成长。

On June 29, UCACF hosted its Impact Reception during the Fifth Chinese American Convention. Donors, grant recipients, UCACF board members, and community leaders gathered to reflect on the impact of UCACF-supported projects across the country.

The reception featured updates on UCACF's strategic direction and grant program, while grant recipients shared their achievements and the community stories behind their projects. The event was both an expression of gratitude to UCACF's longtime supporters and partners and an opportunity for community organizations to connect, exchange ideas, and explore future collaboration.

Behind every grant is a community idea beginning its journey from vision to action. UCACF looks forward to connecting more donors, nonprofit organizations, and community partners so that more meaningful initiatives can take root and grow.

❄️ 讲述华裔故事，共庆美国建国250周年

为庆祝美国建国250周年，UCACF今年在拉斯维加斯谷地区组织并支持了一系列面向社会公众的文化活动。

系列活动包括电影《Far East, Deep South》展映与讨论会、在克拉克县政府大楼举行的"Enduring Legacy: Chinese Americans and the Making of Nevada"历史展览，以及"America 250: Melting Pot"主题音乐会。

这些活动通过讲述华裔美国人的历史与故事、展示中华文化，促进华裔社区与不同族裔之间的交流与理解，让更多公众看见华裔群体为美国社会发展作出的贡献。

与此同时，《伊利华报》推出美国建国250周年主题版面，以"告别时代，期待和平"为题，从历史与当代视角出发，表达华裔社区对美国历史、社会发展与和平未来的关注和思考。

To commemorate the 250th anniversary of the United States, UCACF organized and supported a series of public cultural programs across the Las Vegas Valley.

The programs included a screening and community discussion of the documentary Far East, Deep South; the historical exhibition "Enduring Legacy: Chinese Americans and the Making of Nevada" at the Clark County Government Center; and the "America 250: Melting Pot" concert.

By sharing Chinese American history and experiences while showcasing Chinese culture, these programs encouraged dialogue and understanding between the Chinese American community and people from diverse backgrounds. They also helped more members of the public recognize the important contributions Chinese Americans have made to the development of the United States.

The Erie Chinese Journal also published a special America 250 feature under the theme "Farewell to an Era, Hope for Peace." Through both historical and contemporary perspectives, the feature reflected the Chinese American community's thoughts on American history, social change, and the hope for a more peaceful future.

🎭 "致敬赛珍珠·维加斯戏剧周"圆满落幕

"致敬赛珍珠·维加斯戏剧周"在拉斯维加斯圆满收官。活动期间共呈现四场历史人物话剧《赛珍珠》、三场儿童舞台剧《悟空北美历险》及多场华语脱口秀演出，同时举办了由UCACF资助的《98封私信》新书发布会。

从赛珍珠跨越中美的人文情怀，到孙悟空充满想象力的北美历险，再到高二牛、赵汉黎带来的青春脱口秀，各项活动受到拉斯维加斯当地居民及第五届美国华人大会嘉宾的广泛好评，也成为当地华人社区热议的话题。

此次演出也是北美枫香话剧的第130场演出。来自五个州的演职人员携手完成了这场跨越地域、年龄与文化的艺术交流之旅。

The "Pearl S. Buck Tribute · Las Vegas Theater Week" concluded successfully in Las Vegas. The program featured four performances of the historical play Pearl S. Buck, three performances of the children's stage production Wukong's Adventures in North America, several Chinese-language stand-up comedy shows, and a UCACF-supported book launch for 98 Letters.

From Pearl S. Buck's enduring connection with China and the United States to the imaginative North American adventures of the Monkey King, as well as the youthful comedy performances of Gao Erniu and Zhao Hanli, the programs received enthusiastic responses from Las Vegas residents and guests attending the Fifth Chinese American Convention. They also became widely discussed topics within the local Chinese American community.

The week also marked the 130th performance presented by North American Fengxiang Theater. Cast and crew members from five states worked together to complete a meaningful cultural journey that crossed geographic, generational, and cultural boundaries.

🎬 致敬语言艺术大师乔榛

由UCACF支持的"第三届国际华语原创IP电影节暨首届纽约AI国际影展"组委会日前专程赴上海，为著名配音艺术家乔榛先生颁发"终身成就奖"，向其多年来在语言艺术和影视配音领域作出的杰出贡献致以崇高敬意。

乔榛先生用声音塑造了无数经典角色，也让一代代观众通过语言艺术感受到电影的温度与力量。

此次活动不仅是对一位杰出艺术家的致敬，也体现了电影节鼓励华语原创、传承语言艺术并推动国际文化交流的宗旨。

With support from UCACF, representatives of the Third International Chinese-Language Original IP Film Festival and the Inaugural New York AI International Film Festival recently traveled to Shanghai to present renowned voice artist Qiao Zhen with a Lifetime Achievement Award. The honor recognized his outstanding contributions to language arts and film dubbing throughout his distinguished career.

Through his unforgettable voice performances, Mr. Qiao has brought countless classic characters to life and enabled generations of audiences to experience the warmth and power of cinema through the art of language.

The presentation was not only a tribute to an exceptional artist but also reflected the festivals' commitment to supporting original Chinese-language works, preserving the art of spoken language, and promoting international cultural exchange.
